## 2. Project Purpose

This project is a personal website and portfolio for Micael Zecsen Saragih.

The website is intended to:

1. Establish a clear and authentic personal presence on the web.
2. Present Micael's identity as a Computer Science student.
3. Showcase projects, technical interests, activities, and personal development.
4. Demonstrate both technical and interpersonal capabilities.
5. Support Micael's application and personal positioning for opportunities such as the Google Student Ambassador program.
6. Provide a foundation that can continue to grow as Micael gains projects, experience, certifications, achievements, and professional experience.

The website must represent Micael accurately. It must not present him as more experienced or skilled than the available evidence supports.

Micael has been interested in technology since childhood.

Because of limited economic circumstances, he initially had limited access to technology and relied heavily on a mobile phone to explore and learn about technological developments.

His current journey is focused on continuously learning, experimenting, and building technology projects while developing communication, leadership, adaptability, and other personal skills.

## 18. Design Philosophy

The website should prioritize:

> **Personal identity over visual gimmicks.**

The design should make visitors understand who Micael is, what he builds, what he is learning, and what kind of person he is.

Avoid turning the website into a generic "developer template".

Avoid excessive:

- Neon effects
- Random gradients
- 3D elements
- Particle effects
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Large decorative animations
- Unrelated visual effects

Visual effects must support the identity and content.
